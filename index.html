I am an Assistant Professor at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, in Civil Engineering and in the Institute for Sustainable Development.

My research starts from one premise: the energy transition is, above all, a materials problem.

I build quantitative models to understand our future material needs, how circularity can reduce that demand, and who bears the environmental and health costs in a clean energy future.

My goal is to provide qualitative recommendations towards a sustainable energy transition.
